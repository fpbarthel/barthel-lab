I am a PhD student in the Neuroscience program at Arizona State University. I graduated from Arizona State University in May 2023 with a major in Neuroscience and a minor in Business. My previous research in the Gewirtz lab at ASU was centered on uncovering the epigenetic mechanisms underlying individual differences in addiction susceptibility.

I am very excited to continue my research in Neuroscience, while shifting to include a more functional genomics focus in exploring health and disease.

Outside of the lab, I spend a lot of my free time doing Jiu Jitsu, Muay Thai, and playing guitar. The remaining time I have is spent with friends, family, and my dogs.
