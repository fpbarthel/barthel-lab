The lab traveled to Melbourne, Florida for the [FASEB Science Research Conference on the Consequences of Aneuploidy](https://events.faseb.org/event/Aneuploidy/summary) (September 28 – October 1, 2026), with a selected short talk and three posters:

- **Short talk** (Floris): *Break-induced replication repositions ribosomal DNA and alpha-satellite arrays to chromosome ends in human ALT cancers*
- **Poster** (Griffin): *KaryoScope: alignment-free in silico cytogenetics detects clinically relevant structural variants in acute myeloid leukemia and multiple myeloma*
- **Poster** (Mimi): *Telomere dysfunction in human astrocytes drives acrocentric chromosome instability and nucleolar reorganization*
- **Poster** (Daniel): *Alignment-free resolution of chromatin organization across repetitive DNA in telomerase- and ALT-positive cancer cells*
